Prayer companions

Each one grows when you post a prayer or pray for someone.

Pip the Lamb

Lambkin → Little Lamb → Lamb of Light. Gains a halo at Level 3 and sparkles at Level 5.

Olive the Dove

Her olive branch grows a leaf each level, and her tail trails light from Level 5.

Judah the Lion Cub

Starts with a fuzzy little mane that fills out and turns golden as he levels up.

Ember

A vigil candle that burns taller and brighter with each level. A natural fit for the candlelit vigil.
